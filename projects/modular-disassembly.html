Remote Disassembly of Electronics-Free Modular Structures

We developed a load-bearing modular structural system that can selectively disassemble without onboard electronics. A single remote vibration source transmits a mechanical signal through the assembled structure, while connector geometry determines when and how each connection releases.

01

The Engineering Challenge

A reconfigurable modular structure needs its connections to perform two seemingly conflicting functions.

The connections must remain mechanically stable during assembly, transportation, and load-bearing operation. Selected connections must also release on command without requiring a motor, controller, sensor, or power source at every joint.

Can connector behavior be programmed through geometry so that it remains stable under normal loading but releases when a remote vibration signal reaches a designed threshold?

02

System Architecture

The system is built from cuboctahedral modules assembled from repeated planar components. Each module uses two connector systems with separate mechanical functions: one for structural assembly and one for programmable release.

Module assembly visual

Intra-Connectors

Structural assembly

Intra-connectors join six repeated planar components into a three-dimensional cuboctahedral module.

  • Tool-free push-in assembly
  • Self-locking geometry
  • Reusable friction-based connections
  • Load-bearing structural stability
  • Fabrication from one repeated part type
Connector release visual

Inter-Connectors

Programmable release

Inter-connectors join neighboring modules and determine when the assembled structure separates.

  • Stable mechanical locking during use
  • Geometry-controlled release threshold
  • Vibration-dependent effective friction
  • Stored elastic energy drives separation
  • No onboard actuator or electronics

03

Programming Mechanical Behavior through Geometry

The connector geometry controls two independent mechanical outputs: the vibration power required to trigger release and the elastic energy released after detachment.

P* Critical vibration power

Determines when the connector releases.

Er Released elastic energy

Determines how far the modules separate.

By changing the connector tip geometry and tentacle thickness, these two responses can be tuned separately. Two connectors can release at the same critical power while producing different separation distances.
