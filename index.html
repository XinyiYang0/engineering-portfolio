Engineering Projects

Programmable modular structures that selectively disassemble using a single remote vibration source

Large modular structures can be assembled from repeated components, but selectively taking them apart usually requires human intervention, robotic manipulation, or actuators and electronics embedded throughout the structure.

We developed a load-bearing modular structural system that can selectively disassemble without onboard electronics. A single vibration source transmits a mechanical signal through the assembled structure, while the geometry of each connector determines when and how it releases.
